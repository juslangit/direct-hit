# UI surfaces

Generated on OpenArt (Seedream 4.5, 2K, 15 credits each) on 2026-09-18, in the
painted-naval direction Luqman chose for the key art.

| file | what it is | what it is used for |
|---|---|---|
| `chart_paper.jpg` | Aged cream chart paper, fold creases and foxing | The plotting table's surface |
| `steel_plate.jpg` | Brushed blue-grey naval steel | Buttons and panels |
| `brass_trim.jpg` | Worn brass with patina | Rules, borders and edging |

All three were asked for as plain seamless textures. The steel came back with
panel seams across it and the brass with ribs and screws, so a clean region was
cropped out of each rather than re-rolled - a button background wants an even
surface, and a trim strip wants a single band. The paper came back exactly as
asked and is used whole.
